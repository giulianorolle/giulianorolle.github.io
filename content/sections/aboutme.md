I am a **PhD student in Applied Economics** at the [Gran Sasso Science Institute](https://www.gssi.it) (GSSI) in L'Aquila, Italy, supervised by **Alessandro Palma** (GSSI) and **Ilan Noy** (GSSI and Victoria University of Wellington).

My research is in **environmental economics**, **health economics** and **applied microeconometrics**, with secondary interests in education and labour economics. My dissertation studies the **health and human-capital effects of environmental exposures**, from prenatal exposure to **pesticides** to **wildfire smoke**.
